agent-link-converter demo

Paste a Taobao, Tmall, Weidian or 1688 product URL. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

Example config: examples/our-sites.config.js holds the referral codes used on the 18 agent spreadsheet sites run by this project's owner. If you tick it, the owner may earn a referral commission. Leave it unticked to get links without any referral code.
